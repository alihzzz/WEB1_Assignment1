Cristiano Ronaldo

CR7 | GOAT of football

About Cristiano Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro (born February 5, 1985) is a Portuguese professional footballer who plays as a forward. Renowned for his athleticism, goal-scoring ability, and dedication to physical fitness, he has established himself as an iconic figure in global sports history.

Ronaldo has won five Ballon d'Or awards and four European Golden Shoes, the most by a European player. Over his professional career, he has scored over 850 official goals for both club and country, winning numerous domestic league titles and Champions League trophies.

Key Career Milestones

  1. 2002: Made his professional debut at Sporting CP in Lisbon.
  2. 2003: Signed with Manchester United and won his first Premier League titles.
  3. 2008: Won his first UEFA Champions League title and first FIFA Ballon d'Or.
  4. 2009–2018: Joined Real Madrid, scoring 450 goals and winning 4 Champions League titles.
  5. 2016: Led Portugal to victory in the UEFA European Championship (Euro 2016).

Major Achievements & Records

  • All-Time International Goalscorer: Record holder for most international goals scored.
  • UEFA Champions League Record: All-time top scorer in Champions League history.
  • Ballon d'Or Winner: 5-time recipient of the prestigious world player award.
  • 5 World Cups Scored: First male player to score in 5 different FIFA World Cup tournaments.
